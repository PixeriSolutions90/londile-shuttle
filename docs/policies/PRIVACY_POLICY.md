## 1. Introduction

Londile Shuttle ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, how long we keep it, who can access it, and how you can request deletion of your data.

- Registered users (with login)

**Important:** We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), 2013 (Act No. 4 of 2013), the primary data protection law in South Africa.

## 2. What Personal Information We Collect

### 2.1 Guest Booking Information
When you make a booking without creating an account, we collect:
- Full name (first and surname)
- Contact number (phone)
- Email address (if provided)
- Residential address
- Booking reference number (auto-generated, not personal)
- Trip dates (operational, not personal)
- Verification code (operational security)

**Why:** To process your booking and contact you about your shuttle trip.

### 2.2 Registered User Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address (login credential)
- Password (encrypted, you control this)
- First name and surname
- Contact number
- ID/Passport number
- Residential address(es) you save
- Booking history (linked to your account)

**Why:** To manage your account, provide personalized service, and keep a record of your bookings.

### 2.3 Agent Information
When you register as a shuttle agent, we additionally collect:
- Company name
- VAT/Tax number
- Company registration number
- Company address
- Business contact details
- Booking history (for their clients)

**Why:** To verify your business, manage your operations, and track bookings you create.

### 2.4 Administrator Information
Admins have all user-type data access plus:
- Admin action logs (what you do in the system)
- User management history
- System audit trails

**Why:** To manage the platform and investigate issues.

### 2.5 Automatically Collected Information
- Login timestamps
- IP address (for security)
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited on our platform
- Device information

**Why:** To operate the platform securely and improve user experience.

## 6. How Long We Keep Your Data

### 6.1 Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason | Disposal Method |
|-----------|------------------|--------|-----------------|
| **Guest bookings** | 7 years after completion | Tax compliance (SARS), booking history | Hard delete from database |
| **User accounts** | While active + 1 year after deletion | Legal hold, dispute resolution | Hard delete from database |
| **Agent records** | While active + 2 years after termination | Business records, auditing | Hard delete from database |
| **Payment records** | 6 years (if applicable) | Tax and legal compliance | Anonymized then deleted |
| **Audit logs** | 1 year | Security and incident investigation | Deleted automatically |
| **System backups** | 30 days | Disaster recovery | Purged automatically |

### 6.2 What "Delete" Means
When we delete your data:
1. Removed from production database
2. Removed from all backups after 30 days
3. Encrypted keys destroyed
4. Not recoverable by our team

## 7. Your Rights (POPIA Rights)

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the following rights:

### 7.1 Right to Access
You can request a copy of all personal information we hold about you.
- **How:** Email [support@londile-shuttle.co.za](mailto:support@londile-shuttle.co.za) with subject "DATA ACCESS REQUEST"
- **Timeline:** We respond within 20 business days
- **Cost:** Free for first request, R50 per additional copy

### 7.2 Right to Correction
You can update or correct inaccurate information.
- **How:** Log into your account and edit your profile, OR email your corrections
- **Timeline:** We correct within 5 business days

### 7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
You can request deletion of your personal information.
- **How:** Email [support@londile-shuttle.co.za](mailto:support@londile-shuttle.co.za) with subject "DATA DELETION REQUEST"
- **Timeline:** We process within 20 business days
- **Limitations:** We may retain data if:
  - Required by law (tax records, court orders)
  - Dispute resolution is ongoing
  - Anti-fraud investigation is active

### 7.4 Right to Object
You can object to how we process your data.
- **How:** Email [support@londile-shuttle.co.za](mailto:support@londile-shuttle.co.za) with specific objection
- **Timeline:** We respond within 20 business days

### 7.5 Right to Data Portability
You can request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).
- **How:** Email [support@londile-shuttle.co.za](mailto:support@londile-shuttle.co.za) with subject "DATA PORTABILITY REQUEST"
- **Timeline:** We provide within 20 business days

## 8. How to Request Data Deletion

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Send Email**
   - To: [support@londile-shuttle.co.za](mailto:support@londile-shuttle.co.za)
   - Subject: "DATA DELETION REQUEST"
   - Include: Your full name, email, and account ID (if registered)

2. **We Verify Your Identity**
   - We confirm you are who you claim to be
   - May request government ID copy

3. **Review for Legal Holds**
   - Check if data is needed for:
     - Active disputes
     - Ongoing investigations
     - Tax compliance (7-year hold for bookings)

4. **Delete or Anonymize**
   - Delete data if no holds apply
   - Anonymize if retention is required (remove name, contact, but keep booking number)

5. **Confirmation**
   - We email confirmation when deletion is complete
   - Usually within 20 business days

### What We CAN'T Delete

The following data cannot be deleted (retained for compliance):
- Booking records older than [X years] (tax requirement)
- Payment records (6 years - tax compliance)
- Court-ordered data holds
- Ongoing dispute-related data

**However:** We can anonymize it (remove your name, contact, ID; replace with "DELETED_USER_XXX")
